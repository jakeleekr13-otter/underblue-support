Why UnderBlue

  • Pay once, no subscription: UnderBlue Pro is $14.99 on iPhone and $19.99 on Mac, paid once.
  • Private by design: photos and videos are processed on your iPhone or Mac. No account, no upload.
  • Made only for color: photos get depth-aware correction. Pick a look, then fine-tune it with Custom sliders.
  • Video stays intact: frame rate and audio are kept. UnderBlue Pro exports full-length and 4K video.
  • A whole dive at once on Mac: drop in a folder of photos and videos and correct them in one batch.
  • Free to try: no sign-up needed.

AquaColorFix

A one-tap color fix for photos and video. It has many manual sliders, such as hue, exposure, shadows and black point. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Source: AquaColorFix on the App Store

Dive+

A diving community app with a color fix built in. You also get dive logs, dive-site search and buddies from around the world. Good if you want one app for logs and photos. Full use needs a paid plan.

Source: Dive+ on the App Store, Google Play

DeepColors

An automatic color fix for photos and video, with keyframes for video grading. Premium adds 4K, HDR and ProRes export and LUT export. It is a subscription.

Source: DeepColors on the App Store, DeepColors website

DiveClick

One-tap presets made by underwater photographers, with simple controls for warmth, tint, exposure, contrast and saturation. Exports without a watermark need a subscription.

Source: DiveClick on the App Store, DiveClick website

Aqura FX

A free web tool. It runs in the browser on a phone or computer, so there is nothing to install. It can batch a whole dive to a ZIP file and export video with sound.

Source: Aqura FX website

UnderBlue

Made only for color. For photos, it estimates depth in the scene and corrects near and far parts differently. Videos keep their frame rate and audio. Processing stays on your device. The iPhone app and the Mac app are separate. The Mac app corrects a whole folder in one batch. Both are free to try. UnderBlue Pro is paid once, with no subscription.

Which one should you choose?

  • You want natural color back and no subscription: UnderBlue.
  • You want to fix a whole dive on a computer: UnderBlue for Mac, or Aqura FX in the browser.
  • You want a community and a dive log, and a subscription is fine: Dive+.
  • You edit on iPad: AquaColorFix or DeepColors.
  • You want video grading with keyframes and LUT export: DeepColors.
  • Not sure? Start with UnderBlue. It is free to try.
