AquaColorFix

A one-tap color fix for photos and video. It has many manual sliders, such as hue, exposure, shadows and black point. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Source: AquaColorFix on the App Store

Dive+

A diving community app with a color fix built in. You also get dive logs, dive-site search and buddies from around the world. Good if you want one app for logs and photos.

Source: Dive+ on the App Store, Google Play

UnderBlue

Made only for color. For photos, it estimates depth in the scene and corrects near and far parts differently. Videos keep their frame rate, audio and HDR. Everything runs on your iPhone. It is free to try. UnderBlue Pro costs $14.99, paid once, with no subscription.

Which one should you choose?

  • You just want natural color back: choose UnderBlue. It is made only for color and runs on your iPhone. Start free, then pay once: $14.99 for UnderBlue Pro.
  • You want a community and a dive log: Dive+.
  • You edit on iPad or Mac today: AquaColorFix.
  • Not sure? Start with UnderBlue. It is free to try.
