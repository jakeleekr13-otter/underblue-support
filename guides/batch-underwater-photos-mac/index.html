Fix the color of a whole dive on your Mac, in one batch

After a dive trip you may have hundreds of blue photos and many videos. Fixing them one by one takes hours.

UnderBlue for Mac corrects a whole folder at once. This guide shows how.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26 or later.
  • UnderBlue for Mac from the Mac App Store. It is a separate app from UnderBlue for iPhone.
  • Your dive photos and videos in a folder.

Step 1: Add your files

Drag files or whole folders from Finder into UnderBlue. Photos and videos can go in the same queue.

Step 2: Pick a look

Choose Natural Dive, Tropical or Deep Dive. Or use Custom and set five sliders yourself. Check the before and after preview for any item.

Step 3: Correct and save

Choose the folder for the results, then press Correct. UnderBlue works through the queue and saves each result there. Your original files are not changed.

Tips for a big batch

  • Sort by depth or by dive. Shallow and deep photos often look best with different looks.
  • Try the look on a few files first. Then run the whole folder.
  • Keep the originals. You can run them again with another look later.

Free and Pro

You can try one photo export and one 10-second video for free. UnderBlue Pro is a one-time purchase of $19.99 on the US Mac App Store. It unlocks batch processing, unlimited photos, full-length video and supported 4K and HDR export.

Private by design

Processing stays on your Mac. There is no account and no cloud upload.
