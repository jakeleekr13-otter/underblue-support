UnderBlue Support

Help with underwater photo and video restoration on iPhone.

Getting started

  1. Open UnderBlue and choose Select Photo or Select Video.
  2. Select a look and adjust its intensity.
  3. Use Compare to check the original.
  4. Choose Export, then save the completed result to Photos.

Common questions

Why can an export take time?

UnderBlue performs depth-aware restoration locally on your iPhone. Long, high-resolution, or HDR videos can take longer and may require additional free storage.

Why is HDR unavailable?

HDR export is offered only for a compatible HDR source after UnderBlue verifies that the device can create a valid HDR output. SDR export remains available.

How do I restore UnderBlue Pro?

Tap Get Pro on the home screen, then choose Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Diagnostics

Open the information button on the UnderBlue home screen to prepare a privacy-safe diagnostic report. Nothing is uploaded automatically. You decide whether and where to share the report.

Contact support

Email support@enheart.me for private support, including help with sharing a diagnostic report.

For non-sensitive questions, you may also open a public UnderBlue support request.

Do not attach photos, videos, diagnostic files, email addresses, or other private information to a public GitHub issue. Start with a brief description of the problem; a private method for sharing diagnostics can be arranged if needed.

Privacy Policy

UnderBlue is designed for recreational diving memories and is not a scientific colour-measurement or professional grading tool.
